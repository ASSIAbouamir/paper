\section{Conclusion \& Design Principles}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This paper investigated why adaptive routing architectures struggle against stationary fusion in dual-expert sequential recommendation, establishing the governing empirical realities and boundary conditions of multi-expert fusion:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Why Sequence-Level Routing Fails Against Static Fusion:} Across both compact ($d=64$, $\text{HR@10} = 0.0515$) and high-capacity backbone pairs ($765.2$ hits, $\text{HR@10} \approx 0.127$), sequence-level scalar routing fails to outperform stationary fusion. Summary uncertainty features lack candidate-level directional resolution, inducing collateral false-positive tail intrusions on the consensus majority. Rather than strictly collapsing to identical uniform weights, learned routers fluctuate within the sampling noise of static fusion without extracting significant specialist signal.
    \item \textbf{The Disagreement Penalty Principle and Its Boundary Conditions:} In compact consensus-dominant regimes ($89.6\% H$), candidate-level geometric arbitration (CC-PoE) recovers modest headroom over Static Logit PoE ($0.0725 \to 0.0765$, $+24$ net hits on canonical Seed 42, $+18.2$ mean hits across 5 seeds). However, our 5-pair high-capacity benchmark transparently demonstrates that the fixed disagreement penalty does not transfer to stronger backbone pairs (mean $\Delta = -17.2$ hits across 5 retrained pairs), because isolated specialist opportunities are scarce ($n_{01} \approx 42.0$) while collaborative hits put at risk are substantial ($n_{10} \approx 201.6$).
    \item \textbf{Sparse E-Commerce \& Non-Parametric Dominance:} On Amazon Beauty, our post-hoc diagnostic audit demonstrates that unlearned non-parametric co-occurrence combined with content centroids (\texttt{Item-kNN + Sim}, $723.0$ hits) vastly outperforms untuned neural sequence Transformers ($168.7$ hits). Neither CC-PoE ($283.3$ hits) nor Soft MoE ($206.0$ hits) improves over simple fusion, illustrating the sensitivity of off-the-shelf neural sequence models on ultra-sparse catalogs without domain-specific hyperparameter search.
    \item \textbf{Persistent Latent Headroom as an Open Challenge:} While discrete oracle headroom exists, closing this gap without incurring collateral losses on strong collaborative predictions remains an open problem that requires item-aware confidence estimation rather than global divergence penalties.
\end{enumerate}

Our investigation reframes multi-expert fusion from an unconstrained neural routing problem to a rigorous examination of stationary baselines, establishing clear boundary conditions for when and why adaptive routing can---or cannot---improve over simple fusion.
